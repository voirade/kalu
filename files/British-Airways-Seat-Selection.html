channels. Seat availability and pricing can vary according to the flight, fare, cabin, and

Important Steps to Select a British Seat
Open your British Airways reservation or begin a new booking.
Review the available seat map for your flight.
Select your preferred available seat.
Review any applicable seat-selection charge.
Confirm the seat assignment and updated itinerary.
💺
When Choosing a Seat
Seat availability can change before departure. Travelers should review the seat map and
applicable terms before confirming the selection. Some seats may have different features or
pricing depending on the cabin and fare.
